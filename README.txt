Abhaya Nursing Buddy - clickable redesign prototype

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser with a phone frame on the right
   and every screen listed on the left. Click buttons inside the phone to move through the app.
3. On a phone, open index.html and tap "Open the app".

HOW TO SHARE IT WITH OTHERS
- Easiest: go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
  You get a public link anyone can open on their phone or laptop, no sign-up needed to view.
- Or upload the folder to GitHub and turn on GitHub Pages, or send the zip itself.

NOTES
- Internet is needed only for the Figtree font; everything else works offline.
- Screens are plain HTML files (*.dc.html). Styles live in assets/abhaya.css.
- Home (desktop) is a 1280x800 layout and opens in its own tab.
