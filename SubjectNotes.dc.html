Heartchambers and valves

Four chambers

The upper atria receive blood; the lower ventricles pump it out. The left ventricle is thickest, pushing blood to the whole body.

The four valves

Valves keep blood flowing one way. Tricuspid and mitral sit between atria and ventricles; pulmonary and aortic guard the exits.

Remember it"Try Pulling My Aorta": Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral, Aortic, in order of flow.

On the ward

A murmur often means a valve isn't opening or closing fully. Listen for it when assessing heart sounds.
